Developing Better Futures

Adeer International is recognized as a prominent gateway for global investors from Saudi Arabia and the Gulf area. Our services go beyond the conventional real estate paradigms, encompassing alternative investment consultancy, acquisitions, financial services, transactional facilitation, valuation expertise, property management, leasing solutions, technology and more. Known for our proficiency in corporate acquisitions, Adeer International remains committed to crafting success stories across diverse landscapes and circumstances, a testament to our unwavering dedication to excellence and encompassing our dedication to our heritage.
 
Adeer International

It all started in 2008

Our story is a testament to the power of perseverance and dedication. It all started in 2008 when we began our journey with Sumou Holding Company, initially known as Sumou Real Estate, and subsequently evolving into Adeer Real Estate in Saudi Arabia inspired by founder Dr. Ayedh Al Qahtani and directed & executed by Mr. Meteb Hamed Al Saad CEO, Adeer Real Estate thrived. Over time, Adeer Real Estate seamlessly transitioned into Adeer Holding, giving rise to Adeer International, with a global presence in the United Kingdom, Egypt, Turkey, and Switzerland. Serving as the quintessential conduit for Sumou Holding & Adeer Holdings’ ventures and investments beyond Saudi Arabia.

Our global presence..

Our influence has crossed borders and extended the roots from our home country the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia to other dynamic locations such as the United Kingdom, Egypt, Switzerland, and Turkey with a strategic plan to be present in 45 countries around the globe over the coming 10 years. This expansion indicates the company’s adaptability and extreme potential for growth. By establishing presence in diverse markets, we have positioned ourselves as the leading players in the international real estate industry, leveraging our expertise, innovation, and unwavering commitment to excellence to succeed on a global scale.
